🔒 Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us.

1. Introduction

CreateLK Academy respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when using our website, learning platform and related services.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how we use it, how we protect it and how third-party services such as payment providers, hosting providers and advertising services may process information.

2. Information We Collect

When you create an account, use our learning services, make a membership request or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where provided
  • Account login information
  • Learning and course progress information
  • Membership information
  • Payment or transaction information
  • Payment verification documents, where submitted
  • Information you provide when contacting us

We only seek to collect information that is reasonably necessary for providing and managing our services.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide educational and learning services
  • Track learning progress and course activity
  • Manage membership subscriptions
  • Verify payments and membership requests
  • Provide certificates or other learning-related services
  • Respond to questions and support requests
  • Improve our website and educational services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorized access
  • Maintain the security and reliability of our services

4. Payment Information

CreateLK Academy may use third-party payment services or manual payment verification processes for membership purchases.

When payment verification is required, we may receive or process transaction-related information such as payment reference details, transaction amount, date and other information necessary to verify the payment.

We do not intentionally collect unnecessary payment information. Where a third-party payment provider processes a payment, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website may use cookies, browser storage, local storage or similar technologies to maintain login sessions, remember preferences, improve website functionality, understand website usage and provide relevant services.

Some cookies or similar technologies may be placed or used by third-party services that operate on our website.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies or browser storage may affect some website features, including login or personalization functions.

6. Advertising and Google Services

CreateLK Academy may use third-party advertising services, including Google advertising services such as Google AdSense, to display advertisements on our website.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve advertisements based on a user's previous visits to this website or other websites.

Google's advertising cookies may allow Google and its advertising partners to show advertisements that may be relevant to users based on their visits to websites and other information permitted under applicable advertising policies.

Users may learn more about Google's advertising practices and manage certain advertising personalization settings through Google Ads Settings .

Users may also learn more about how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services through Google's privacy resources.

7. Third-Party Services

Our services may use third-party providers for hosting, databases, payment processing, authentication, analytics, advertising, file storage or other technical functions.

These providers may process information necessary to provide their services. Their processing of information may be governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services when using features provided by those services.

8. Information Sharing

We do not intentionally sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers when reasonably necessary to operate the website, process payments, provide hosting, maintain security, provide advertising services or deliver other requested services.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law, legal process or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, services or organization.

9. Account Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account information and other information handled through our services.

However, no website, online service or method of transmitting information over the internet can guarantee complete security. Users should also take reasonable steps to protect their passwords and account information.

10. Children's Privacy

CreateLK Academy provides educational content for general learning purposes.

Users should provide accurate information and, where required by applicable law, obtain appropriate parental or guardian permission before using our services.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

11. Data Retention

We may retain account, learning, membership, payment and support information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer reasonably required, it may be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.

12. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights or choices regarding their personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of certain information.

Users may also manage certain browser cookies and advertising preferences through their browser settings and available third-party controls.

Requests concerning personal information can be made through our Contact Us page.

13. External Links

Our website may contain links to external websites or services. CreateLK Academy is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of websites that we do not operate.

Users should review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technologies, legal requirements or privacy practices change.

Updated versions will be published on this page. Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for changes.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the way we handle information, please contact CreateLK Academy through our Contact Us page.

16. Important Notice

This Privacy Policy is intended to provide transparent information about our general privacy practices. It does not replace legal advice and may need to be updated according to the specific services, technologies, advertising partners and legal requirements applicable to CreateLK Academy.
