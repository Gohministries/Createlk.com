Who We Are

CreateLK Academy is an online English learning platform designed to help students and learners improve their English skills through simple, practical and accessible learning resources.

Our goal is to make English learning easier by providing structured courses, grammar lessons, vocabulary practice and interactive MCQ exercises.

What We Offer

📖 English Courses

Practical lessons covering grammar, vocabulary and everyday English.

📝 MCQ Practice

Interactive questions to help learners test and strengthen their knowledge.

🤖 AI Practice

Premium learners can practise with AI-generated English questions.

⭐ Premium Learning

Additional learning features designed for learners who want more practice.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help learners develop practical English skills and become more confident in communication, education and future opportunities.

We believe English learning should be simple, practical and available to everyone.
