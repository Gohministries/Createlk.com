Module Overview

Welcome to the first step of your Facebook creator and monetization journey. Before earning any revenue, you must configure your online presence correctly and establish the right profile structure.

Key Steps to Begin:

  • Switch to Professional Mode: Turn your personal profile or create a dedicated Creator page optimized for public reach.
  • Complete Your Bio & Branding: Ensure your profile picture, cover photo, and bio clearly state what niche or content you provide.
  • Verify Location & Eligibility: Check that your account is registered in a region supported by Meta's features and that your personal details match your payout profile requirements.
  • Review Community Standards: Familiarize yourself with basic compliance rules to ensure your early posts do not trigger accidental flags.

Next Action

Once you have your profile properly configured, proceed to the next module to plan out your content strategy and consistency goals.
